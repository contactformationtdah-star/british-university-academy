READING PRACTICE · B2

Why do universities value independent learning?

At university, students are expected to take greater responsibility for their learning. Lectures introduce key ideas, but students usually need to explore topics further through independent reading, research and discussion.

Independent learning does not mean studying alone all the time. It also involves asking questions, evaluating sources and exchanging ideas with classmates. These skills help students become more confident and thoughtful learners.
